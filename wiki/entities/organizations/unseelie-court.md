# Unseelie Court

## Overview

Through these gates, the Unseelie Court entered and grew thorns, weeds, and feral predators in the Queen’s dream world. While the Summer Queen and the Frost Prince's eternal rivalry has seen peace for some time, the Unseelie Court has begun steps to overthrow the Summer Queen, and taken their war to the human world. Sophrenia was recently killed in a fight with a dark elf priest from the Fey Wild, and the Resistance is desperately searching for whoever betrayed her to the Unseelie Court.

## Beliefs

- While other Elves have turned to worshipping Spirits and even Gods, the Bladesingers honor the tradition of the Lady of the Sidhe. _(source: [[raw/runesite/destruction-of-the-elves.html.md|raw/runesite/destruction-of-the-elves.html.md]])_
- Part of the Llygoden - a secret society. _(source: [[raw/runesite/old-notes.html.md|raw/runesite/old-notes.html.md]])_
- The elves are steeped in magic, worship the Spirits, and have an army of warrior-wizards called the Bladesingers Guild. _(source: [[raw/runesite/old-notes.html.md|raw/runesite/old-notes.html.md]])_
- Religion Many humans, and nearly all of the Fey, worship the Spirits of the Elements - elemental beings. _(source: [[raw/runesite/old-notes.html.md|raw/runesite/old-notes.html.md]])_

## Practices and Structure

- While the Elves helped the Lady and the Eladrin fight off the attacks of the Fomorian king and the Summer Queen, they were quickly forgotten and eventually ordered to leave the environs of the Eladrin city of Senaliesse. _(source: [[raw/runesite/destruction-of-the-elves.html.md|raw/runesite/destruction-of-the-elves.html.md]])_
- Sophrenia was recently killed in a fight with a dark elf priest from the Fey Wild, and the Resistance is desperately searching for whoever betrayed her to the Unseelie Court. _(source: [[raw/runesite/old-notes.html.md|raw/runesite/old-notes.html.md]])_
- The Dwarven clans are: Boulderfist (a decimated warrior clan), Cuil (priests and artisans), Stonehinge (craftsmen and builders), Battlehammer (another warrior clan that has recently risen in power since the decimation of Boulderfist), and Aramar (the former clan of Kings, but since the Decimation, no one has claimed the throne). _(source: [[raw/runesite/old-notes.html.md|raw/runesite/old-notes.html.md]])_
- Their priests manifest their powers in keeping with the beings that they worship. _(source: [[raw/runesite/old-notes.html.md|raw/runesite/old-notes.html.md]])_
- The other major religion is the Church of the Great Creator , which recently finished an enormous temple in the heart of Atania. _(source: [[raw/runesite/old-notes.html.md|raw/runesite/old-notes.html.md]])_
