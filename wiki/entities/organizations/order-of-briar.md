# Order of Briar

## Overview

They eschew the Order of Briar, for unknown reasons, but otherwise are a strong mix of Church of the Creator adherents and followers of the various elemental religions. The Order of Briar . In rural or small towns, the ancient Order of Briar, formerly the Order of the Grave, is remembered for caretaking graveyards.
