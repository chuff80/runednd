# Church of the Creator

## Overview

The largest city in the Domhaine an Duine, the Kingdom of Atania was home to The Engineering School and the Church of the Creator . There was a massive influx of Goblin acolytes into the Church of the Creator, spawning a new order of Thaumaturges. The Church of the Creator is the largest faith group in the land, having spread to every corner of the continent and beyond.
