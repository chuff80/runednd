# Cumhnantach

## Overview

The Cumhnantach knew how to tap the lines of this language of power to do what was necessary to accomplish their goals. The Cumhnantach didn’t use that much power without discussing it in advance. Members of the Cumhnantach were allowed to defend themselves with their hard-earned powers.
