# Dragon Talkers

## Overview

From the chaos of Ghealdar arose an unlikely group of heroes named The Dragon Talkers. One of the Dragon Talkers and friend to Briar, the Saint of the Church of the Creator. She flew from there with the Dragon Talkers to Ghealder, where she worked with them to confront and defeat The Raven Queen.

## Beliefs

- They will impose order on the world in the name of the Creator, and the great powers of the world are too distracted by the coming apocalypse to help the mortals recover from the elemental chaos. _(source: [[raw/runesite/Calendar.html.md|raw/runesite/Calendar.html.md]])_
- The party discovered that Banrion Fitheach, the Raven Queen's true name, was an arch-djinn imprisoned by the Celestial Choir in the name of the Creator. _(source: [[raw/runesite/Calendar.html.md|raw/runesite/Calendar.html.md]])_
- The battle started off in terrifying fashion with the Raven Queen unleashing powerful magic spells that inflicted massive damage to the entire party - with the exception of Briar, the new cleric of the Creator, who had mysteriously disappeared. _(source: [[raw/runesite/Calendar.html.md|raw/runesite/Calendar.html.md]])_
- The Faith Crisis is long in the past. _(source: [[raw/runesite/Calendar.html.md|raw/runesite/Calendar.html.md]])_
