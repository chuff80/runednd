# Celestial Choir

## Overview

Their cousins, the mountain dwarves from The Dragonspine, sent emissaries Ragnom and Magdek Blessedarmor , both raised to Aasimar by the Celestial Choir because of their righteousness and relationship as cousins of Sadavir, as well as Khardan Tothgin and Gralmyr Gurdarunn, Clerics of the Creator . Meanwhile, the Celestial Choir prepares for war as they cannot tolerate the chaos that has been unleashed. The party discovered that Banrion Fitheach, the Raven Queen's true name, was an arch-djinn imprisoned by the Celestial Choir in the name of the Creator.
