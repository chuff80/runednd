# Sidhe Court

## Overview

The Summer Queen predicted that Niko's change would bring chaos to the Sidhe Court, but this level of destruction caused the Lady to banish her to the mortal realm out of frustration. To overcome the coup, Gabalifix had called upon help from the Sidhe Court and the Winter Queen herself. But those interested in seeing humanity survive had extracted concessions from the Summer Queen and other members of the Sidhe Court.
