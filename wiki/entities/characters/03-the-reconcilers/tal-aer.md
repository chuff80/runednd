# Tal'Aer

`Canon status:` This page is a curated synthesis from canon runesite extracts. The canonical entity name is `Tal'Aer`; one major history source uses the longer form `Tal'Aeronwae`, treated here as the same figure pending contradictory evidence.

`Era anchor:` The Reconcilers (695 AG)

## Overview

Tal'Aer is recorded as a high-impact elven bladesinger and gate-guardian figure tied to post-crisis stabilization across the mortal realm and Feywild. Canon history credits Tal'Aeronwae as a key Reconciler-era actor who helped open Scribhan Doras (the Runegates), expand cross-realm communication and trade, and support the long peace that followed. Separate canon race-history text attributes Tal'Aer with negotiating peace between Orc and Elf powers and establishing an elf settlement arrangement within the Kingdom of Gibard/Hartland.

## Appearance

Current canon extracts emphasize role and historical consequence over physical description. Tal'Aer is characterized primarily through title and function: elite bladesinger lineage, gate-guardian expertise, and diplomatic authority in high-conflict interspecies negotiations.
