# Aidan

`Canon status:` This page is a curated synthesis from campaign notes and story drafts. Aidan's timeline has conflicting reports; uncertain points are called out.

## Overview

Aidan appears as a pivotal and morally volatile figure in the Atania resistance arc: a former paladin of the Church of the Great Creator, a wartime organizer, and at times a feared enforcer. In different notes he is both collaborator and resistance ally, with his decisions tied closely to Kaufman's rise, covert operations in Atania, and internal betrayal in resistance networks. Narrative material also presents a younger Aidan as a devout but impulsive squire whose crisis of faith deepens after personal losses and prolonged violence.

## Appearance

Most accounts present Aidan as a young but battle-worn human fighter whose bearing reflects formal church martial training. In resistance-era scenes he is typically armed and moving in practical field gear rather than ceremonial insignia, with demeanor and posture shifting from zealous confidence to visibly strained vigilance as losses accumulate.

## Working Timeline

1. **Church and martial formation (early period)**
Aidan is framed as a young religious fighter, training as a squire in the Order/Knights of the Creator and treating his actions as obedience to divine will.
Source trail: [[raw/stories/The Unkindness of Ravens Full.doc.md|The Unkindness of Ravens Full]], [[raw/runesite/old-notes.html.md|Old Notes]].

2. **Kaufman alignment and covert buildout (Atania power period)**
Aidan publicly breaks with church leadership, aligns with Kaufman, and is described as running or training a paramilitary/shadow espionage force with assassination capability.
Source trail: [[raw/runesite/old-notes.html.md|Old Notes]], [[raw/oldnotes/RJ's backstory.docx.md|RJ backstory]], [[raw/oldnotes/Old Skool D&D Party Run Down.doc.md|Party Run Down]].

3. **Internal fracture and fall from grace**
Later notes depict Aidan as disillusioned, claiming Kaufman had effectively overtaken Liam and compromised resistance structures; Aidan then provides shelter and protected routes to party members.
Source trail: [[raw/oldnotes/Old Skool Session Notes 11.17.11.docx.md|Session Notes 11.17.11]], [[raw/oldnotes/Session Notes 1.4.12.doc.md|Session Notes 1.4.12]].

4. **Disappearance, contradictory sightings, and unfinished consequences**
Some accounts say Aidan disappeared with Kaufman; others report him helping resistance operations against the Empire. The fate of people affected by his covert actions (especially Marie) is unresolved.
Source trail: [[raw/runesite/old-notes.html.md|Old Notes]], [[raw/oldnotes/RJ's backstory.docx.md|RJ backstory]].

## Character Arc and Themes

- **Faith vs. trauma**: Early devotion to the Creator shifts toward anger, doubt, and instrumental violence under pressure.
- **Protector vs. coercer**: Aidan both shelters allies and allegedly runs coercive covert operations.
- **Control vs. conscience**: He appears to oscillate between Kaufman-linked command structures and attempts at moral correction.
- **Public role vs. hidden role**: Publicly visible break with church; privately linked to shadow networks and safe-house logistics.

## Capabilities and Methods

- Military/religious combat training (paladin/squire background).
- Paramilitary training and covert tradecraft instruction.
- Safe-house logistics and clandestine movement inside Atania.
- High willingness to escalate under pressure.
- Strong motivational language rooted in duty, faith, and survival.
