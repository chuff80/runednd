# Religieuse

`Canon status:` This page is a curated synthesis from runesite session/history extracts. `Religieuse` is the canonical entity name; `Juice` appears as an in-text alias in session narration.

`Era anchor:` The Ghealdar Goblins (1350 AG)

## Overview

Religieuse is portrayed as an unusually large goblin adventurer associated with Rutherford's circle and major field actions between Ghealdar and Atania. Session records repeatedly cast him as a heavily armored support anchor: healing allies under pressure, controlling tempo in chaotic fights, and sustaining group survival during maritime and subterranean operations. His characterization combines practical care work (notably cooking and provisioning) with frontline resilience, making him both a morale center and a combat stabilizer for his team.

## Appearance

Canonical descriptions present Religieuse as an enormous goblin with a booming presence, commonly seen in heavy armor and shield. He is depicted as physically imposing yet coded as clumsy/soft in contrast to his twin brother Sarge, and is repeatedly linked to kitchen craft and baked food motifs ("biscuits," pastries) alongside battlefield magic.
