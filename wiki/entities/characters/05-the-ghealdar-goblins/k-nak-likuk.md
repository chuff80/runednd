# K'nak'likuk

`Canon status:` This page is a curated synthesis from session-note extracts. The operational profile is strong; naming and alias overlap with "Click Clack/Klick Klack" should be treated as canon-adjacent until formally resolved.

`Era anchor:` The Ghealdar Goblins (1350 AG)

## Overview

K'nak'likuk is consistently portrayed as a versatile shapeshifter and utility specialist within Religieuse's party network. Across the source sessions, he repeatedly takes non-human forms (including dog, octopus, wolf spider, and warhorse-like mounts) to solve movement, reconnaissance, and survival problems under pressure. The same notes place him at key turning points in both surface-city extraction and subterranean high-risk combat, marking him as one of the group's main adaptive tacticians.

## Appearance

The sources emphasize form-shifting behavior over a fixed baseline physique, so visual continuity is intentionally unstable in-session. He is most reliably characterized by rapid transformation choices and battlefield improvisation rather than static physical descriptors.
