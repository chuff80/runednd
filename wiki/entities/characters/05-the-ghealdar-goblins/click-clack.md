# Click Clack

`Canon status:` This page tracks a named party figure from session logs. Source spelling varies ("ClickClack," "Click Clack," "Klick Klack"), and overlap with K'nak'likuk is unresolved in current extracted canon.

`Era anchor:` The Ghealdar Goblins (1350 AG)

## Overview

Click Clack appears in the same mission chain as Religieuse, Sarge, and Beau during the Ghealdar slave-rescue and Atania transition arc. Session notes repeatedly depict Click Clack using transformational mobility and shock action at decisive moments, including infiltration support and mounted-impact combat forms. Because naming conventions fluctuate across notes, this page preserves the character thread as a standalone entry while documenting strong potential overlap with K'nak'likuk.

## Appearance

Like K'nak'likuk's profile, this figure is represented through transformations rather than fixed presentation. The logs emphasize function over form: quick adaptation into animal or cavalry-like bodies to create openings, escape pressure, or break enemy lines.
