# Beau

`Canon status:` This page is a curated synthesis from session-note extracts. Species and role are well attested; finer biography details should be expanded only when additional primary sources are added.

`Era anchor:` The Ghealdar Goblins (1350 AG)

## Overview

Beau is presented as a young goblin traveling with Religieuse's wider team during the Ghealdar-to-Atania arc. Session notes frame him as both a political flashpoint and a practical combat contributor: he is repeatedly tied to contract/slavery disputes in Ghealdar, then appears in coordinated ship and dungeon actions where he provides offensive spellcasting. The same source trail places him in the Boneclub household sphere in Atania, indicating he is not just a transient ally but a key connective character between goblin freedom politics and arcane institutions.

## Appearance

Direct physical description is limited in the extracted notes, but Beau is consistently framed as a younger goblin among more battle-hardened companions. Narrative emphasis is less on physique and more on social position, magical capability, and the pressure of being publicly claimed, contested, and defended.
