# Sarge

`Canon status:` This page is a curated synthesis from session and location extracts. Core identity points are well supported; chronology details remain open to refinement.

`Era anchor:` The Ghealdar Goblins (1350 AG)

## Overview

Sarge appears as a frontline goblin actor in the Ghealdar slave-rescue storyline and is explicitly connected to Religieuse through family ties. Session notes show him requesting operational help, guiding the team into hostile spaces, and participating directly in rescue and escape actions. Broader setting text then anchors his identity as a major goblin figure shaped by the city's slave economy and post-slavery instability.

## Appearance

Canonical Ghealdar text describes Sarge as one of the "enormous goblins" and contrasts him with Religieuse: where Religieuse is softer and clumsier in characterization, Sarge is described as muscular beneath his bulk. This physically imposing profile aligns with his role in direct action scenes.
