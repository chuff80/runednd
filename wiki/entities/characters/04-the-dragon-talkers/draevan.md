# Draevan

`Canon status:` This page is a curated synthesis from runesite canon pages. Source spelling varies between "Draevan" and "Draeven"; this page uses "Draevan" as the primary canonical label with documented alias variance.

`Era anchor:` The Dragon Talkers (1380 AG)

## Overview

Draevan is listed among the Dragon Talkers who rose from the Ghealdar crisis and entered the anti-Raven-Queen campaign. In the final battle account, the same character appears with variant spelling ("Draeven") and is identified as the party ranger whose battlefield arrow volleys significantly degraded enemy forces. This places Draevan in the group of members with explicit tactical contribution detail in the canonical climax sequence.

## Appearance

Sources do not provide detailed portrait-level physical description. Role framing emphasizes ranged combat presence and battlefield control, consistent with ranger characterization.
