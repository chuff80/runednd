# Cog

`Canon status:` This page is a curated synthesis from runesite canon pages. Core identity notes are explicit in battle-era sources; broader life history is limited.

`Era anchor:` The Dragon Talkers (1380 AG)

## Overview

Cog is listed as a Dragon Talkers member in the 2019-2021 Raven Queen campaign arc. Calendar canon gives Cog one of the clearest tactical profiles in the party: a wizard figure described as originally an animated suit of armor and later reforged into a legendary sword. During the final battle, Cog is credited with chain-lightning strikes that cleared remaining enemy forces. Ghealdar canon also places Cog in postwar movements with Asher and Sadavir.

## Appearance

Unlike most members of the roster, Cog's presentation is partly metaphysical/mechanical in canon language. Sources emphasize a transformed state ("animated suit of armor" to "legendary sword"), suggesting that Cog's visual identity is tied to artifact-like embodiment rather than ordinary mortal anatomy.
