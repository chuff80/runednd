# Asher

`Canon status:` This page is a curated synthesis from runesite canon pages. Asher's party role is well attested; detailed biography remains sparse in currently extracted sources.

`Era anchor:` The Dragon Talkers (1380 AG)

## Overview

Asher is named as one of the Dragon Talkers who emerged from Ghealdar's instability to broker high-stakes peace between goblin rebellion leaders, the Ghealdaran Elders Council, and Gibard orc leadership. In the Raven Queen war narrative, Asher is identified as one of the party's frontline fighters during the final confrontation. Canon text also places Asher in the postwar Ghealdar aftermath alongside Sadavir and Cog before disappearing in pursuit of Darian Bloodstone-linked history.

## Appearance

Primary sources focus on battlefield function rather than visual detail. Asher is framed as a martial frontline combatant whose role centers on direct melee pressure in the party's decisive engagements.
