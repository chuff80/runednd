# Sadavir

`Canon status:` This page is a curated synthesis from runesite canon pages. Sadavir is strongly attested as a Dragon Talkers combat lead; lineage and postwar details are present but still fragmentary.

`Era anchor:` The Dragon Talkers (1380 AG)

## Overview

Sadavir appears as one of the Dragon Talkers central to late-era peace and war actions around Ghealdar. Calendar canon identifies Sadavir as one of the two primary frontline fighters in the final confrontation with the Raven Queen. Additional sources connect the "Blessedarmor" family name to Sadavir and situate him in postwar Ghealdar conditions with Asher and Cog, reinforcing his role as both battlefield actor and consequential regional figure.

## Appearance

Specific physical detail is limited, but combat framing presents Sadavir as a heavy melee specialist using axe-forward fighting in the highest-intensity phase of the Raven Queen war arc.
