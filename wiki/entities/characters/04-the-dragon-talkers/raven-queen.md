# Raven Queen

`Canon status:` This page is a curated synthesis from runesite canon pages. Sources consistently identify the Raven Queen with `Banrion Fitheach` (an arch-djinn), but differ in moral framing between apocalyptic villain, tragic seeker of release, and postwar symbol.

`Era anchor:` The Dragon Talkers (1380 AG)

## Overview

The Raven Queen is a campaign-defining antagonist of the 2019-2021 era, culminating in the Raven Queen's War and the Great Destruction that follows her defeat. Canon history ties her rise to factional conflict in Ghealdar, mass mobilization by Dragon Talkers-era heroes, and the revelation that she is Banrion Fitheach, an arch-djinn imprisoned by the Celestial Choir. Her final arc combines catastrophic warfare, necromantic and spiritual imagery, and an endgame bargain that secures her destruction while unleashing long-suppressed elemental powers across the world.

## Appearance

Descriptions emphasize battlefield terror and magical force more than fixed physical detail: she appears as an overwhelming war-mage figure attended by minions and soul-linked sustain effects. Later religious and civic memory preserves her image through aftermath language (vengeance, undead devastation, crow/raven symbolism) rather than portrait-level anatomy.
