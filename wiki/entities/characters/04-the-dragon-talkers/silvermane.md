# Silvermane

`Canon status:` This page is a curated synthesis from runesite canon pages. Silvermane has stronger individual detail than most Dragon Talkers members due dedicated mention in Gibard records.

`Era anchor:` The Dragon Talkers (1380 AG)

## Overview

Silvermane is a Dragon Talkers member associated with the Raven Queen war and remembered in orcish lore as a major heroic figure. Canon sources identify Silvermane as both a core Dragon Talkers participant and a close ally of Briar. In Gibard memory, Silvermane is elevated to near-foundational status among modern orc heroes, linked to narratives of renewal, military resilience, and post-catastrophe identity.

## Appearance

Direct visual detail is limited, but Silvermane is framed through high-heroic martial imagery in orc-centered canon narratives. Text also associates Silvermane with rumored early blessing by Al-Abyad, signaling a mythic or spiritually marked presentation in later retellings.
