# Gabalafix

`Canon status:` This page is a curated synthesis from mixed-era canon and draft narrative sources. The name is spelled inconsistently in sources (`Gabalifix`, `Gabolifix`, and `Gabalix`); this page follows the requested `Gabalafix` label and treats those spellings as the same figure.

`Era anchor:` The Gaeas Falls (Recorded History begins (0 AG))

## Overview

Gabalafix is presented as a long-lived minotaur emperor and military strategist whose occupation of Atania defines one of the setting's major oppression-and-resistance eras. Canon site material frames him as the ruler tied to martial law, imperial expansion, and the historical trauma still remembered in Atania's institutions and memorials. Narrative sources deepen that portrayal by presenting him as tactically brilliant, personally formidable, and politically linked to Winter Court objectives around control of Ogham access and human subjugation.

## Appearance

In narrative depictions, Gabalafix is nearly eight feet tall, horned, and heavily muscled, with rich brown fur and a commanding battlefield presence. He is repeatedly shown carrying a massive two-handed sword, favoring austere presentation over ornament, and projecting authority through voice and posture rather than ceremony.
