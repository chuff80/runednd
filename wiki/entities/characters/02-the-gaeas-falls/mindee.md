# Mindee

`Canon status:` This page is a curated synthesis primarily from story-draft material. Core identity beats are consistent, but sequence details and naming shifts (`Mindee`, `Dubhaine`, `Caile`) are presented with unresolved internal contradictions.

`Era anchor:` The Gaeas Falls (Recorded History begins (0 AG))

## Overview

Mindee is portrayed as a lethal Tuatha-linked assassin used by the Cumhneantach to enforce the Gaeas by hunting "blasmaigh" humans who rediscover Ogham practice. Narrative sources repeatedly show her as both an external threat to Sophronia and Liam and an internalized survival persona within a fractured identity system centered on Dubhaine/Caile. Over time, her role shifts from executioner to conflicted collaborator as she breaks with Cumhneantach methods and helps train Atanian resistance cells against imperial occupation forces.

## Appearance

Mindee is usually depicted as short, fast, and visibly Tuatha-marked by pointed ears, often concealed with cloth or hooding during infiltration. She fights in practical, close-combat gear with knives and blades, relying on speed, precision, and Ogham-enhanced movement. Later scenes tied to Dubhaine describe a stubbled scalp, worn field clothing, and a visibly exhausted but disciplined presence after prolonged injury and concealment.
