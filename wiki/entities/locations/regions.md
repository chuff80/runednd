# Regions

## Overview

Dragonshome Peak and Dragonshome Circle - the peak of Dragonshome mountain is where Darian Bloodstone destroyed Cyric the Warlord in the Hartland War. The original legends suggest that Cyric's phylactery was flung from the mountain into the inaccessible Circle upon Cyric's death. Darian's body was laid to rest inside the mountain, and is guarded by the spirit of his dragon mount, Pyraxus.
