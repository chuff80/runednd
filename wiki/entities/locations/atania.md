# Atania

## Overview

The largest city in the Domhaine an Duine, the Kingdom of Atania was home to The Engineering School and the Church of the Creator . The Dao Manmun caused an earthquake that destroyed the miles-long cavern structure underneath Atania (formerly called the Ogafau), causing a great deal of the city to slide into the ocean. King Darian the 10th died in the earthquake, leaving the city without a leader.
