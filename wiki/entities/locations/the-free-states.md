# The Free States

## Overview

After the Reconciliation, the lost noble families of Atania were offered an opportunity to return to Atania and reclaim their ancestral homes. Many members of the tribes opted to establish their own settlements instead of returning to Atania. Over time, the various settlements formed agreements with each other on boundaries and trade.
