# Timeline

`Canon status:` This page is a curated high-level chronology synthesized from canon timeline pages and event references; exact dates remain approximate where sources disagree.

## Overview

This is the setting-wide timeline anchor for major historical phases and turning points. It is intended as a top-level chronology that links out to detailed event pages such as [[entities/history/hartland-war.md|The Hartland War]].

## Belligerents and Key Figures

- [[entities/characters/darian.md|Darian Bloodstone]] and early Atanian royal institutions.
- [[entities/characters/cyric.md|Cyric the Warlord]] and aligned war factions during the Hartland era.
- Tuatha/Sidhe war leadership networks (including figures like Badb and Celestina in retrospective sources).
- Postwar/reconciler-era actors, including [[entities/characters/liam.md|Liam]] and later Atanian political coalitions.

## Timeline and Turning Points

1. **Foundational Atanian era**: early state formation, cross-realm diplomacy, and pre-collapse institutions.
2. **Hartland War period**: genocidal escalation and civilizational rupture (see [[entities/history/hartland-war.md|The Hartland War]]).
3. **Cyric's defeat and postwar settlement**: Dragonshome endpoint followed by containment politics.
4. **Gaeas age**: long period of memory suppression and fragmented post-imperial identities.
5. **Rediscovery and reconnection era**: mortal-fey recontact, renewed Ogham conflict, and modern resistance/reconciliation cycles.

## Outcomes and Lasting Impact

- History is experienced in layers: remembered myth, partial canon records, and contested survivor narratives.
- Prewar institutions persist mostly as symbols, ruins, and revived traditions.
- War-era identity fractures continue to shape modern politics, religion, and faction alignment.
- Timeline interpretation remains an active strategic question for current leaders and movements.
