I was born in Attania, son of Mariah (43) and Ronald (48). I have 2 other siblings, an older sister, Ann (24), and a younger sister Marie (12).  My older sister was involved in an affair with a mysterious stranger, Aluin, that nobody in our family had met.  She disappeared when I was twelve and it was many years later that I had contact with her.  I came of age around the time Kaufman came into power.  Filled with a sense of nationalism, I joined the paramilitary force that Aidan was training.  Aidan discovered that my strengths lay in espionage and intrigue.  I was one of the lieutenants in his shadow army that only he and Kaufman were aware of.  I performed regular sentry duties, but occasionally I was given covert tasks and missions that mostly involved recon and the occasional assassination.  I was contacted by my sister before Aidan and Kaufman fled.  Apparently, Aluin, now her husband, is involved in some kind of resistance movement.  Ann was able to open my eyes concerning Aidan’s and Kaufman’s corruption, and I became a double agent.  After many months of serving as Aluin’s catspaw in the shadow army, I finally met my sister’s husband.  I didn’t think that elves existed but I saw him with my own eyes!  There is this whole other world filled with elves, some good, some bad.  Aluin helped me to see the big picture, and I was eager to throw my lot in with him.  I regularly spied on Aidan, and was eventually discovered.  I was able to escape, but Aidan went to my home, and took Marie.  I went to Aluin for help and suggested that it would be safer for myself and my parents to live under Rasseter’s protection.  Aidan and Kaufman fled the city, or at least can not be found.  I still have heard no word on where my little sister Marie is..
