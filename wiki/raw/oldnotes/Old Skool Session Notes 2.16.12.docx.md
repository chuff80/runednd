Session Notes 2.16

Party decided that they needed to figure out who the mole was. They were able to meet with Aluin inside of a Resistance safe house and discuss what the next plans were. 

They met Aluin’s assistant, Guinevere. A slim, darkly dressed secretary-ish thief who runs errands for Aluin. She’s the actual traitor. 

The party has decided that they can either: 

	•	Laeryn, one of Sophrenia’s closest friends, has a mother being held hostage by the Empire. She helps run the spy network, focusing on the children. 
	•	The party is feeding information to her to see if it leaks out. 
	•	Lariat, one of Rasseter’s right-hand men, is amassing his own power base. Storing weapons, food, and money. Nobody knows quite how much, but he’s obviously getting help from somewhere else. He’s actually an Oni. 
	•	The party decided to go interrogate him, and ended up attacking him. I decided that he’d let Rasseter know the party attacked him and that Rasseter wouldn’t be too pleased with it. 
	•	The Black Ravens could be smuggled into the city? If they’re given weapons when they get in…? 
	•	Weapons manifests – 5 captains, 80 – 100 soldiers each. The cache supplies up to 100 soldiers, but there could be two additional caches. 
	•	Guinevere, Ann’s friend, and Aluin’s assistant, is the actual traitor. She’s around from here on out, and the party should see her a lot. 

When the party gets back from fighting with Lariat, Rasseter will be pissed that the party attacked him. He will threaten and bully them until they agree to undertake a difficult and dangerous task to make up for what they did. 

They are to attack another troop of Empire soldiers while other members of the Resistance raid the prison. They’re working to free several bruisers who they can set free to continue murdering Empire troops. 

At the same time this raid happens, the Black Raven tribe invades the city. The Empire soldiers “accidentally” leave the gates open and the barbarians are able to invade the city and start pillaging. The entire city becomes a disastrous hell hole, dissolving into roaming battle after roaming battle. The barbarians start killing the low level soldiers as well as the commoners. 

The Empire will wait several days or weeks before they bring out the Ogres, Minotaurs and Drow to put down the invading army. In the interim, they’ll make a show of fighting with the invaders and protecting citizens. They invade houses to build defenses, and press people into dangerous situations in the name of defense, but they’re really getting rid of enemies.
