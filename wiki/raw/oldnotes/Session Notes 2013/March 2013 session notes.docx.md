Lariat invited the party to help the goblins. 

Blood king of Atania – Liam – has a contract with the goblin nation. Goblin leaders signed a contract to serve the King of Atania. 


The fight does not go well for the party. Just when things get to their worst, Brock will hear a voice telling him to pull out the mace and hold it aloft, invoking the Spirit of Wind, Gaoth. When he does this, his powers will surge and everyone will get a healing surge & second wind immediately. 

A short lived teleportation circle power will be given to Brock, and he will be able to open a gate to the Feywild, where they will meet an agent of the The Lady in a Fey city called Senaliesse. When they step through the portal, Nia the Monk will meet them. She will introduce the party to Saeliae, a handmaid of the Lady, who will shelter the party and introduce them to the Lady’s generals, wizards, and other powerful entities. 

Ashenport is where the library of contracts between the Fey and mortals. The Lady wants the party to find out about the contract with the Goblins. 

Everyone should be at level 6

Lariat tells the party about the Goblin contracts with the Empire and the King of Atania. Brok has a personal interest in what happened with Liam, getting him to become the rightful king of Atania. 

On the day after all of this happens, the PDX party is going to raid the palace. 

The Old Skool party should be given a quest by The Lady
