Old Skool D&D Party Run Down

Jared – Brok (Dwarf Cleric) – Refugee with the rest of the dwarves who were killed by the Betrayal in the Square. Kade (Brok’s grandfather) was one of the Elder’s Council killed by Kaufman for opposing him. He is good friends with the mangy dog that is Liam’s permanently polymorphed grandfather. In possession of a magical hammer that belonged to his grandfather. He is the fulfillment of Darian’s blessing on his ancestor that her child would become valiant and pure. 
Spencer – Ash (Longtooth Shifter Runepriest) – Silver Wolf Tribe barbarian. A warrior tribe full of strong hunters. Their tribe was poisoned by the Black Raven tribe and they disbanded into small groups, united again by a glorious Fey being pretending to be a God. This drove Ash away and he was given powers by a strange being in the woods. He arrived in Atania shortly thereafter. 
Katherine –  Kayleth (Elven Druid) – Grew up in the Hartland Forest, sent to Atania to help the Elves help the humans against the Empire. 
Ben – Randolf Justin Stinger (Human Thief) – Sister had an affair with a figure named Aluin who is a member of the Resistance. Was trained as a paramilitary spy by Aidan, but turned double agent by Aluin. Marie, his sister, was kidnapped by Aidan after he found out about the betrayal.  
Nicholas – Wynne Shadoweye (Halfling War Cleric) 
Justin – Jariana (Human Fighter) – Barbarian warrior slave from a tribe taken over by the Black Ravens.
