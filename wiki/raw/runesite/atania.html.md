The largest city in the Domhaine an Duine, the Kingdom of Atania was home to The Engineering School and the Church of the Creator .

The Dao Manmun caused an earthquake that destroyed the miles-long cavern structure underneath Atania (formerly called the Ogafau), causing a great deal of the city to slide into the ocean. King Darian the 10th died in the earthquake, leaving the city without a leader. Grok , the goblin engineer and powerful wizard, survived and claimed leadership by virtue of his power. He led the creation of a new form of government, a democratically elected parliament.

Before what some are calling the Elemental Apocalypse or Great Destruction, the city was massive. In the Post-Destruction era, Atania languished for a century as various factions fought to establish dominance and the nascent legislature struggled to rule the city-state. Finally, after Gibbard imposed order, the descendants of Grok and the surviving noble houses were able to begin rebuilding Atania as a trading port and center of magical and technological advancement.

With a population of just under one million at last census, housing long ago mushroomed outside the ancient and famous walls. The sprawl, where most of the poorest residents live, was mostly burned to the ground by the undead army brought against the city by the Raven Queen, but has now returned in the form of more permanent housing built by the vast and diverse people of Atania.

Atania's economy is robust and diverse, primarily driven by international trade and large-scale manufacturing. The Bay of Atania is deep and enabled the city to function as the primary trading port with the island nations and other continents for centuries, although Ghealdar's flooding and the changes to the Great River have given Ghealder certain advantages for nations approaching from the East. The Goblin Engineering Guild, founded by Goblin Wizard Grok, has turned Atania into a manufacturing hub. Many of the greatest innovations in Rune have their origins in the Guild, including magically-powered anti-gravity wagons, mass-manufacturing of wands, and glow-bulbs that provide light at all times.

A subterranean city

While the collapse of the caverns killed tens of thousands, many Atanians survived. After Manmun departed, they remade their homes inside the caves, on top of the rubble, and on the plains of the Thir outside the city. The underground homes allow Atanians to stay hidden and avoid the roving street gangs that formed.

The Maw - a large hole opened underneath the old Market Square, connecting directly to the underground cavern system. The markets rebuilt there. Most Atanians use The Maw as a way to get in and out of The Underground. Despite centuries of exploration and mapping, there are many undocumented and unexplored collapsed sewer lines and cavern passageways.

The Temple of the Great Creator

The temple was built on solid foundations, in an area that was not directly over the limestone caverns. It did not collapse. As such, the church of the Great Creator survived. Some Atanians resented this fact, but many saw this as a sign that the Creator is real and a religious revival began among Atanians who survived, and continued to this day.

There was a massive influx of Goblin acolytes into the Church of the Creator, spawning a new order of Thaumaturges. See the Religions section for more information about the church.

Spirit Worship

The common people see the return of the Djinn as the return of vengeful Gods. The Djinn are more apparent and powerful than the Creator, and there's always been hedge wizards and acolytes of the Old Ways, so the people see the Old Ways as ascendant.

It is common to see trinkets and offerings left out for the Djinn, as well as for the brownies, nixies, pixies, and other creatures.

Important Districts and Landmarks

Castle Atania , informally known as Bloodstone Palace, home to the Parliament and the ancestral home of Darian Bloodstone and his descendants.

The Goblin Enginering Guild was the second tallest structure in Atania at five stories high. With the collapse, the tower broke into pieces and slid partially underground. The tower was rebuilt and is once again the tallest building except for Castle Atania. Many of the Engineers privately maintain that the underground chambers make it the tallest and most important building.

The Great Houses in Western Atania were the homes of the Bloodstones and their cousins, the original scions of the current noble families. The houses were rebuilt after the Great Destruction. Not all of the families survived, and others rose to ascendancy over the centuries.

The Spirit Altar in Eastern Atania, alongside the small Sidhe Pool in Serenity Park, is famous for being a place of peace and communion with many spirits.

The Soggy Duck Inn , founded by a small group of adventurers under the guidance of Prince Riardan of the Feywild, features a barkeep who is an animated Walella bush that walks on its roots and speaks in a high pitched, quavering voice. The inn is currently the only active tavern near the Maw, and draws a small clientele - mostly powerful travelers in disguise.

Gabalifix Memorial . At the base of Castle Atania, there was a large statue of the minotaur Emperor Gabalifix with his hoof on the head of a little boy. It stands as a warning of what might come if the people ever forget to be vigilant about their safety and their freedoms. The statue was lost and restored after the Great Destruction.

Ogofau . The caves beneath Atania. The caverns and tunnels connect with the city's sewers. The name is derived from the Duergar’s word for Cyric's magic lab.

Important People and Organizations

Manmun's earthquake destroyed the locking mechanism of the Runegate underneath the Castle Atania, allowing Domhaine an Sidhe (the Feywild) beings to come and go freely. It is now heavily guarded by a mix of Goblin engineers and church Thaumaturges.

After the Great Destruction, The Summer Queen came through the portal and brought with her Gridgemoth the Troll , general of her armies. The Summer Queen's touch can be seen intermittently throughout the city, with some plants periodically blooming at odd times and warm spots of sunlight spontaneously appearing. These are called the Summer Queen's touch.

Cyric's Duergar were driven to the surface by the earthquakes. Their cousins, the mountain dwarves from The Dragonspine, sent emissaries Ragnom and Magdek Blessedarmor , both raised to Aasimar by the Celestial Choir because of their righteousness and relationship as cousins of Sadavir, as well as Khardan Tothgin and Gralmyr Gurdarunn, Clerics of the Creator . After the near annihilation of the dwarven clans in the earthquakes, the Blessedarmors sought out their cousins and called down a mighty miracle to cure their leprosy curse. The Duergar and the descendants of the Blessedarmors now occupy a place in regular life in Atania, albeit primarily in the underground.

Prince Riardan , son of the Summer Queen, is a regular guest of the city and emissary to the parliament.

King Darian Bloodstone XV died in the Great Destruction. Atania was founded by King Darian the First. He led the city until the genocidal Hartland War started by his closest friend and advisor, Cyric the Warlord. The ensuing Time of the Gaeas saw a dissolution of the Kingdom. After Liam Bloodstone broke the Gaeas, he resumed the line of kings. The successors of the Bloodstone line have led Atania to become even larger and more prosperous than it was before the Hartland War. The kings had led the country in an unbroken line of succession since then. The Redemption of Liam, a pageant about Liam Bloodstone's triumph over the Fey Court, is performed annually by the teachers of Sophronia's bard college to remind the kings, and the people, of the importance of a king who protected the people from danger, but who is also willing to sacrifice for the people. The current Bloodstone family presides over the Parliament and are seen as important figureheads in the city-state.

Llygoden

A secret society devoted to keeping watch over the Kingdom of Atania. Founded by Fael and Thaede , pre-Reconciliation founders of a resistance movement against Emperor Gabalifix, the Llygoden now exist as a secret society that roots out plots against the Parliament.

Sophronia's Bard College

Founded by the bard Sophronia, an early member of the resistance against Gabalifix's empire, to educate the people of Atania on music, history, and their heritage. Sophronia's Bard College housed and trained bards from all of the races, teaching them the skills that they need to carry out their vital function of lore and communication across the mortal and fey realms.

Sophronia's has been the site of many plots and moments of intrigue within Atania. They maintained an uneasy relationship with the Engineering Guild, as many of the wizards from the Guild accused the teachers from the college of using unorthodox teaching methods and of consorting a little bit too closely with the Summer Court.

The leaders of the college also maintain a memorial for the Plan Stryd – the street children who have died throughout Atania's history.
