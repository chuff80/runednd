The Elves of the Hartland forest have been a part of the forest since the beginning of the world. Part of their creation myth states that when the Fey Queen wanted a hideaway to play in, she dreamed up a world of lush forests full of docile servants. In her dream, these servants accompanied her on hunts, gathering berries, and enjoying the wonders of the world.

In time, the Fey Queen’s enemies discovered her dream world and entered it by creating magical Rune Gates. Through these gates, the Unseelie Court entered and grew thorns, weeds, and feral predators in the Queen’s dream world. The Queen’s servants died to protect her, so she taught them some of her magic so that they might protect themselves from the nightmare that her Dreams had become.

The Bladesingers are the descendants of those servants who learned the Queen’s magic. Her dream songs were lullabies and war chants of such exquisite beauty and magic that they carry the power of an elven sword and spell. Thus, the Bladesingers carry on the tradition of serving the Fey Queen by learning her songs and protecting her world by guarding the Rune Gates against further intrusion.

While other Elves have turned to worshipping Spirits and even Gods, the Bladesingers honor the tradition of the Lady of the Sidhe.

Unfortunately, The Lady has all but abandoned the Sidhe. When the Orc kingdom, led by the Kenku wizard Razorbeak, overran the Hartland forest, the Elves fled into the Feywild. They have remained there for over 500 years, never having the forces necessary to take back their ancestral homeland. While the Elves helped the Lady and the Eladrin fight off the attacks of the Fomorian king and the Summer Queen, they were quickly forgotten and eventually ordered to leave the environs of the Eladrin city of Senaliesse.

The Elves now make their refugee shelter at the base of the mountains in the kingdom of Niko the Frost Prince .
