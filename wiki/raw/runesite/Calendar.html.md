Campaign World History

2021-23, THE REBUILDING OF CIVILIZATION

In the aftermath of the war with The Raven Queen, the arch-djinn moved across Domhaine an Duine, wreaking chaotic destruction. The cliffs beneath Atania have collapsed, sliding much of the city into the ocean, and leaving the Great Houses in ruins, the King dead, and nobody in charge of the salvage effort. The Great River's path was diverted, turning Ghealdar into a newly submerged city. The survivors navigate through the ruins in gondolas poled by the former goblin slaves.

The reclusive dwarven kingdom lost most of its population to earthquakes. The tiny handful of survivors emerged at the edge of Gibard, hoping to find a new home for their people and a reunion with their cursed and diseased Duergar cousins deep underneath the ruins of Atania. Mount Bolcan, on the border of Gibard and Kjeldor, was chosen as the new home of Al-Ahmar, the fire arch-djinn, and the resulting massive eruption destroyed mountain elf villages for miles. While powerful forces closed the Runegate beneath Mount Bolcan in order to save what remained of the fey city of Senaliesse, the lava flow has filled the mountain valleys for miles and burned down miles of the Hartland forest in the North. The same powers that closed the Runegate diverted much of the lava flow to a little known gate in the Desolation, filling miles of the waste with hardened lava and burying an important secret magical library that was an important base for the Cumhneantach - a small cabal of wizards tasked with protecting the balance of magic since the heroes Darian and Cyric walked the land at the dawn of the Age of Mortals.

An ancient group of powerful immortals, including several members of The Reclaimers, have stirred themselves to decide what to do about the arch-djinn. Meanwhile, the Celestial Choir prepares for war as they cannot tolerate the chaos that has been unleashed. They will impose order on the world in the name of the Creator, and the great powers of the world are too distracted by the coming apocalypse to help the mortals recover from the elemental chaos.

The Orcish nation of Gibard rose to power, uniting the various Free States, Ghealdar, and Atania into a single Empire of Gibard that spanned the entirety of the continent.

2019 - 2021, THE RISE OF THE RAVEN QUEEN

The world of Rune (called Domhaine an Duine by scholars) has known over 750 years of peace and prosperity. The Free States and the Kingdom of Atania have grown. The Orc Nation of Gibard in the Hartland forest is flourishing and trade between the nations has built enormous wealth. The trade roads through the Hartland forest into the mountains of Kjeldor have been permanently established. The world is a shining example of what can happen when peace reigns.

The collapse of the goblin slave trade in Ghealdar has left that city-state's economy in ruins. Violence between goblins, orcs, humans and elves is boiling over. Into this mix the orcish nation of Gibard has sent a peacekeeping force. But the Tuatha Da Daanan, the survivors of the conquered elves of the Hartland forest, are waging guerilla warfare and they have brought their violence to the city of Ghealdar as well.

From the chaos of Ghealdar arose an unlikely group of heroes named The Dragon Talkers. Asher, Cog, Muu, Isadorae, Rhaeda, Silvermane, Draevan, and Sadavir came together to aggressively negotiate peace between the goblin rebellion leaders, the Ghealdaran Elders Council, and the Gibard orcs. Their activities set them on a path that discovered a series of young dragons and elves who pledged their allegience to a being eventually known as The Raven Queen.

The party discovered that Banrion Fitheach, the Raven Queen's true name, was an arch-djinn imprisoned by the Celestial Choir in the name of the Creator. At the dawn of time she led a rebellion against the Celestials' attempted ordering of the natural world. Thanks to the imprisonment of the senior arch-djinn, Barqan, Maymun, Al-Abyad, and Al-Ahmar, the Domhaine an Duine has been without the chaos of earthquakes and hurricanes for as long as the memory of mortals. Their return introduced elemental chaos to the world. In order to stop Banrion and the djinn from destroying everything, they determined to end Banrion's conquest.

The final battle started with the party hurtling through the air in a the flying cauldron of Baba Yaga, crash landing in the middle of a battlefield with The Raven Queen, slaughtering hundreds of innocents. The city of Ghealdar was flooded by a river, which was diverted by an earthquake caused by Maymun.

The party immediately began attacking her. They already proved to themselves they could beat her once. They were determined that this time she would not escape them at the end. The battle started off in terrifying fashion with the Raven Queen unleashing powerful magic spells that inflicted massive damage to the entire party - with the exception of Briar, the new cleric of the Creator, who had mysteriously disappeared.

But the party immediately responded. Every member of the party contributed in a significant way. The fighters, Asher and Sadavir, immediately unleashed their greatest moves with sword and axe, inflicting massive damage and drew all of her attention. Draeven, the ranger, rained arrows on the entire battlefield, returning a barrage of damage that nearly incapacitated her minions. Before they died, the shadow elves minions piled on, dragging down Asher and knocking him unconscious. The wizard Cog, originally an animated suit of armor, now reforged into a legendary sword, struck the remaining minions down with a chain lightning spell.

Meanwhile, in the ethereal realm, the cleric Briar was given a vision. She saw the souls of the Raven Queen's dead minions sacrificing their souls to keep her alive. Briar cast a mighty spell that severed the connection between this being of ultimate evil, and the captive souls.

When Briar's spell struck, The Raven Queen realized what happened and immediately began begging for the thing she had been pursuing this entire time: the release of the elemental djinn from their gem prisons. The party agreed, and brought Banrion to the Shadowplane, a mysterious realm that powers the animated undead. Only there could she be destroyed. She willingly allowed herself to be destroyed, finally finding the peace she sought when the party agreed that they would not re-imprison the arch-djinn she had freed.

2015 - 2019 CAMPAIGNS

Magic is now commonplace. The Goblin engineers of Atania have melded technology with magic to create a world where every convenience is easily available for purchase, and many are wealthy enough to live a life of luxury. But that wealth has not reached the goblins of Ghealdar.

The Faith Crisis is long in the past. The actions of the Reconcilers brought down the meddling of the Underworld and Thir Na Nog. The seams of reality are no longer unraveling.

The Church of the Creator is the largest faith group in the land, having spread to every corner of the continent and beyond. Believers can be found in every ethnic and racial group, especially in Atania, the Halfling Hills, and among the Goblin slaves of Ghealdar. The Church and the followers of the Primal spirits are in harmony, though there is agitation as the Primal spirit worshipers dwindle in number.

The world has entered an age of wonder. Because of the Engineering Academy, magic has become commonplace throughout the realms. Every industry has melded technology with magic to make life easier, and to make folk very wealthy. Transportation by magical floating carriage is commonplace. Teleportation is commonly available for the wealthy. Manufacturing has implemented magical means of reproducing tools and goods. Magic is a way out of poverty for smart youngsters.

History

1400 years ago, the Fey war split the Gaeas open and humanity became aware of The Fey, magic, and the Unseen realm.

Seven centuries ago, a small band of reluctant mortals inadvertently stumbled into the even darker secrets behind these earth shattering events. They have attempted to turn away from direct involvement, but the shattering of the Elven Nation and the weakening of the boundaries between the mortal realm, the Feywild, and the Shadow realm have called them to action. The Reconcilers have been called back into action.

Cyric the Warlord showed his hand, revealing himself to be working through his proxy, Liam , a hero of Atania and leader of the Elders Council. These heroes trapped and destroyed the Lich and opened Scribhan Doras - the Runegates into the Feywild. Tal'Aeronwae, the last of the great line of Elven blade singers and gate guardians, ushered in a new era of communication and trade between the Feywild and the mortal realm. Along with the rest of the Reconcilers, Tal'Aeronwae is responsible for the lasting peace of the last 500 years.
