After the Reconciliation, the lost noble families of Atania were offered an opportunity to return to Atania and reclaim their ancestral homes. Many members of the tribes opted to establish their own settlements instead of returning to Atania. Over time, the various settlements formed agreements with each other on boundaries and trade. The Free States send delegations to Atania to negotiate important contracts and settlements.

The Free States outgrew Atania in population. There are hundreds of thousands of folk living all over The Thir, Ghealdar , and the coast.

The Black Raven

Originally the most powerful of the barbarian tribes, the Black Raven house sent several of its members into the horrors of Ghealdar, the City of the Dead, and recovered powerful magical artifacts that pre-dated the tribes on the Thir. The discovery of these artifacts led directly to the closure of the Shadowfell Rift, and the establishment of the Black Raven house as a central power in the Free States and a power to rival the Kingdom of Atania.

Severin Blackraven, scion of the Blackraven family in Darian Bloodstone's day, is still alive as a Death Knight, wandering Ghealdar and vowing destruction of Cyric the Warlord.
