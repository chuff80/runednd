The city of Atania is ravaged by war, and the occupying army, led by a minotaur named Gabalifix , is solidifying its hold on the city, preparing it as a staging area to invade the rest of the continent.

The human armies of Atania had no magic and were woefully unprepared for what they faced. The occupiers aren't cruel, but they have imposed martial law on the city. A small group of humans have staged a resistance, which is secretly being assisted by a coalition of elves. There are all sorts of issues with this assistance, primarily that humans just discovered that elves were real about a year ago.

The underlying cause of all this unrest is the Seelie and Unseelie Fairie Courts using the human lands as a proxy for their immortal wars. For nearly 500 years the Fey Wars raged in the Feywild, contained there by a powerful spell called The Gaeas. This enchantment also kept humans unaware of the Fey happenings in the world around them, oblivious to magic and the occult. Somehow, the Gaeas is weakening. While the Summer Queen and the Frost Prince's eternal rivalry has seen peace for some time, the Unseelie Court has begun steps to overthrow the Summer Queen, and taken their war to the human world.

The City of Atania

The Resistance is made up of:

Rasseter – the leader. He’s been fighting the oppression of the human race for years. For some reason he is immune to the Geaes and sees Fey where others wouldn't normally be able to do so.

Lariat, Fael, and Thaede. Part of the Llygoden - a secret society. These three are also leaders in the resistance.

The Bard College – Laeryn, Sophrenia, Aramar, and Guinevere. Sophrenia was recently killed in a fight with a dark elf priest from the Fey Wild, and the Resistance is desperately searching for whoever betrayed her to the Unseelie Court.

An insane elvish assassin by the name of Aleia . She comes and goes as she pleases, helps with certain kinds of Resistance missions.

Plan Stryd – The Street Children. The eyes and ears of the resistance.

Ogofau – The Caves. The Duergar’s word for Kaufman’s lab, which they won’t go near.

The Hartland Forest

The Elves of the Hartland forest have been a part of the forest since the beginning of the world. Part of their creation myth states that when the Fey Queen wanted a hideaway to play in, she dreamed up a world of lush forests full of docile servants. In her dream, these servants accompanied her on hunts, gathering berries, and enjoying the wonders of the world.

In time, the Fey Queen's enemies discovered her dream world and entered it by creating magical Rune Gates. Through these gates, the Unseelie Court entered and grew thorns, weeds, and feral predators in the Queen's dream world. The Queen's servants died to protect her, so she taught them some of her magic so that they might protect themselves from the nightmare that her Dreams had become.

The Bladesingers are the descendants of those servants who learned the Queen's magic. Her dream songs were lullabys and war chants of such exquisite beauty and magic that they carry the power of an elven sword and spell. Thus, the Bladesingers carry on the tradition of serving the Fey Queen by learning her songs and protecting her world by guarding the Rune Gates against further intrusion.

While other Elves have turned to worshipping Spirits and even Gods, the Bladesingers honor the tradition of the Lady of the Sidhe.

Recently, the Hartland Forest has come under attack by an army led by Razorbeak , a twisted man-bird wizard who was supposedly killed hundreds of years ago, along with Cyric the Warlord during the original Hartland War. Now that Razorbeak is known to be alive, and his personal servants, The Seven Souls, have been seen walking the world, some wonder if Cyric might also be alive.

As Razorbeak's armies of Goblinkind overrun the Hartland forest, the Elves have scattered and fled. A small contingent of elves have managed to escape to the Feywild to seek assistance from the Summer Queen.

Races:

The Dragonborn exist as beings made of magic, created at the whim of the Dragons as their servants and spies, though a handful have joined up with the Empire of Gabalifix as generals in his army.

Dwarves have locked themselves away deep underground in fortresses that no human has ever seen, and only the eldest of elves have seen. Their aversion to sunlight and ability to turn to stone make a dwarf sighting a rare event indeed - and of major importance. The Dwarves have managed, for the most part, to keep themselves from being enmeshed in the political factions of the Seelie and Unseelie Courts, but now that the Feywild is awash in the news of the Unseelie Court's breach of the Gaeas the dwarves may find that they must mobilize to the surface for the first time in centuries.

The Dwarven clans are: Boulderfist (a decimated warrior clan), Cuil (priests and artisans), Stonehinge (craftsmen and builders), Battlehammer (another warrior clan that has recently risen in power since the decimation of Boulderfist), and Aramar (the former clan of Kings, but since the Decimation, no one has claimed the throne).

The dwarves are led by a council of two dwarves from each clan, but their leadership has been less than functional, and major decisions can take many months. The delegation to Atania was five years in the making.

Eladrin are mystical messengers between the Feywild and the mortal realm. They frequent Elven settlements when they are on the Prime material plane, and the city of Atania's importance to the Gaeas has led several important Eladrin to have semi-permanent homes there, living in secrecy among the humans. In the Feywild, the Eladrin inhabit shining cities like Senaliesse, where Summer is eternal.

Several Eladrin have even begun participating in the underground resistance to Gabalifix's invading army.

Tieflings are agents of the Unseelie Court. Most elves, and any Eladrin, will be openly hostile toward a Tiefling and easily provoked into an attack. Tieflings carry out the will of their Unseelie masters on the Prime plane, and any PC would either have to be a renegade trying to engage with the Seelie fey, or a double agent working for the Unseelie.

Elves - the first Fey race to make contact with the humans, the Elves live in the Hartland forest, many miles to the Northeast of Atania. The elves are steeped in magic, worship the Spirits, and have an army of warrior-wizards called the Bladesingers Guild.

The Gnomes are small forest gnomes, very rare and timid creatures who were almost wiped out during the war with the Humans in previous centuries. They mostly keep out of the way and don't get involved in the world.

Orcs & half orcs, with a few exceptions, roam the plains and Eastern swamps, competing with the human barbarian tribes for land.

Halflings are wild and savage, mostly cannibals, who live in a desolate stretch of mountains known as the Spine of the World, defending their homes from the Lizardfolk of the Saeserin swamp, and the strange dark creatures of the new city with Obsidian walls.

Humans , for the most part, live in the city of Atania. Once a large metropolis, the city fell into disrepair at the end of the Hartland war. The warlord Cyric nearly destroyed the city when he fell, and many parts of the ruins were cut off from human awareness by the Gaeas. Now that the enchantment has been lifted, there are unexplored areas in the city where families have lived for generations.

There are also several tribes of barbarians living on the Thir - a great plain separating the human city of Atania from the Hartland forest, where the Elves live. There are also a few small villages of humans on the edge of the Hartland forest, as well as the rumor of nomads living near the city of Ghealdar - a city destroyed by Demons during the Hartland War.

Religion

Many humans, and nearly all of the Fey, worship the Spirits of the Elements - elemental beings. Their priests manifest their powers in keeping with the beings that they worship. The most commonly worshiped spirits are: Gaoth , a spirit of wind; Tintreach a being of energy and electricity; Mearog , a being of stone and earth who only appears underground; Griosagh , a flame spirit blamed for natural disasters and revered by blacksmiths and Aigean , the Ocean.

There are also dozens of lesser known spirits that are revered, feared, or called upon to curse and bless people.  Each of these spirits make up important parts of the day to day lives of the people.

The other major religion is the Church of the Great Creator , which recently finished an enormous temple in the heart of Atania. It's a massive edifice - fortress-like, and it holds not only the clergy, but also the paladins and the monks that were trained there.

The founder of the church, Aemon Ban , recently passed away. Aemon claimed to have traveled beyond the Great Divide to the Gaineamhlach, a great desert, and learned about the Great Creator there. Initially the Church was extremely benevolent, helping the people of Atania become more prosperous. As Aemon aged, however, other church leaders began taking a more day-to-day role and are enforcing a more strict gospel of austerity and self-denial.

Notable recent history

Aidan , a former paladin of the Church of the Great Creator, broke with the church and very publicly fought against his mentors and teachers, siding with a powerful and mysterious human from Atania by the name of Kaufman . Kaufman was, for a time, the leader of the Council of Elders and acted as a king and ruler. When it was discovered, however, that Kaufman had been manipulating the council and murdering people in the completion of dark rituals, both Kaufman and Aidan disappeared. Since the invasion there have been rumored sightings of Aidan working with the resistance to fight the Empire.

It was recently publicly announced that a well respected Tanner and member of the Elder's Council, Darian Bloodstone , had discovered a book of family history that proved that members of the barbarian tribes that wandered the Thir outside of Atania were actually descended from old families in Atania and that their flags and emblems come from old standards that represented once-powerful noble houses.

The Black Raven tribe sent several of its members into the horrors of Ghealdar, the City of the Dead, and recovered powerful magical artifacts that pre-date the tribes on the plains. These artifacts are a major cause of concern for the tribes of the Thir because the Black Raven is using the power to expand their territory and subject smaller tribes.

Other Regions

The Saeserin Swamp - Once home to dozens of tribes of lizard folk, the Saeserin is now an organized nation under the Dead City, a fortress that was built in the heart of the dark jungle. The Dead City is rumored to be an Unseelie Fey outpost or perhaps the source of shadowy beings previously never seen on Rune. Recently, an army of lizard folk invaded the Halfling hills, touching off a major war between the Lizard Folk and the Halflings.

Halfling Hills - While it's true that most halflings are wild cannibals who live on the knife's edge of danger between the barren hills and the horrific things that emerge from the Saeserin. Jaia, the halfling Paladin convert to the Church of the Great Creator, has a sarcophagus that is guarded at all times by her disciples and the Church has a small outpost there. Since the invasion by the lizard folk, the church has become a place where the halflings go to get healing and rest. The Church's small contingent of Wild Boar riding halfling Paladins has been petitioning the church to get involved in the war and it looks like they will win that opportunity.
