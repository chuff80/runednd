Goblins have integrated themselves deeply into Rune society. The goblins who originally entered Atania 700 years ago with the minotaur emperor Gabalifix founded the Atanian Goblin Society of Engineers and the engineer/wizard Grok helped co-found the Wizard Academy. Atanian goblins have strongly shaped the spread of technology and magic across Rune. Artificers work with manufacturers everywhere to help them apply The Sciences to their work.

Around the time of the Cleansing of Ghealdar, three centuries ago, some of the Atanian goblins assisted the early tribes in setting up the Free States. Around the same time, some of the early halfling Church missionaries found great success in preaching to the goblins of the Dragonspine Mountains. Some of those goblins formed a pilgrimage to Atania in order to become priests themselves, or to potentially learn engineering. Early goblins braved terrible hardship through the giant lands to get to Atania. Many of them chose to ride the river to Ghealdar and then take safer roads to Atania, rather than crossing the Thir directly.

When large numbers of goblin pilgrims began showing up in Ghealdar, some of the Free State merchants saw an opportunity. They began offering indentured servitude contracts to the goblins to enable them to bring their families down from the mountains and into Ghealdar and Atania. Because of their poor education these goblins signed contracts that they would never be able to pay off, and which were passed down to their children, now known as the Comain Bocan (the debt goblins), or just Comain .

Some of the current generation of goblins who serve in various capacities in Ghealdar live in their masters homes. Most live in the hovels around the edge of the city. The goblin hovels are unofficially known as The Dump, and many of Ghealdar's citizens haul their trash to the The Dump. The goblins will pick through it, build houses with it, and sell what they can as a way of paying off their family debt.

Atanian goblins look at their cousins with pity. The Church has railed for decades at the dangers of Comain, but the laws of the Free States allow the enforcement of debt with imprisonment or forced servitude. The merchants who own the Comain papers maintain that servitude is better than imprisonment.

Dwarves had locked themselves away deep underground in fortresses that no human saw for centuries. In the last hundred years that has changed as the dwarves have wondered at the advancements of humanity. Their aversion to sunlight and ability to turn to stone make a dwarf sighting a rare event, but one of increasing frequency. The Dwarves have managed, for the most part, to keep themselves from being enmeshed in the political factions of the Seelie and Unseelie Courts, but now that the Feywild is open to travel, a steady trade has emerged. Frozi the Reconciler ensured that the dwarves had normalized relationships with the rest of the world, and now trade flows slowly from the Kingdom Under the Mountain.

The Dwarven clans are: Boulderfist (a small warrior clan that has rebounded since the Hartland War), Cuil (priests and artisans), Stonehinge (craftsmen and builders), Battlehammer (the predominant leadership and warrior clan), and Aramar (the former clan of Kings).

The dwarves are led by a council of two dwarves from each clan, but their leadership has been less than functional, and major decisions can take many months.

Eladrin. The city of Atania’s importance has led several important Eladrin to have permanent homes there and in the Free States. In the Feywild, the Eladrin inhabit shining cities like Senaliesse, where Summer is eternal. With trade between the Feywild and the mortal realm increasing, Eladrin are a common sight throughout Atania and the Free States.

Tieflings are traditionally seen as agents of the Unseelie Court or of the underworld, but since the Reconcilers traveled to Hell with Valichar, the half-demon disciple of the Raven Queen , some tieflings have become accepted members of mortal society.

Elves – the first Fey race to make contact with the humans, the Elves were their staunch allies. Since the Destruction of the Elves , they have been bitter enemies. While Tal'Aer negotiated a peace between the Orcs and Elves, and a settlement inside of Kingdom of Gibard, which the elves insist on calling the Hartland wood, things are tense. Fights regularly break out between elves and orcs. There is a dangerous neutral zone between the settlement of the Kingdom of Gibard.

The Gnomes are small forest gnomes, very rare and timid creatures who were almost wiped out during the war with the Humans in previous centuries. They mostly keep out of the way and don’t get involved in the world.

Orcs & half orcs hail from the Orc Kingdom of Gibard in the Hartland Forest. Gibard was the first leader of the Orcs after the Wizard Razorbeak's death. Originally of the Seven Souls, Gibard went on to forge the Orc nation into a military society that pushed out the forest elves and established hard borders around the forest. In the last 100 year, trade has opened up between Gibard, Atania, the Dwarf kingdom, and the Free States. The mountain elves of Kjeldor have had limited diplomatic relationships, but free trade has not yet happened.

Halflings have mostly abandoned their cannibalistic ways and converted to the Church of the Creator, becoming His most ardent missionaries.

The Dragonborn exist as beings made of magic, created at the whim of the Dragons as their servants and spies. The Dragonborn have a contingent as representatives within each of the major kingdoms. Any Dragonborn players are part of the entourage of these ambassadors.
