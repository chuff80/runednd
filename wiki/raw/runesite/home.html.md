The year is 1935 AG (After the Gaeas). It's been 50 years since the collapse of the orcish Empire of Gibbard . The orcs have retreated into the Hartland forest and are snarled in bureaucratic squabbles that keep them from interfering with other nations.

The independent nations of Rune - Atania , Ghealdar , and the Free States - have begun to stabilize. Peace has been established, and trade between the nations is flowing again. The peace is tenuous, however, and those who seek power are willing to take risks that threaten the collapse of the fragile society.

Atania has re-emerged as a manufacturing and trading hub. Most of the important innovations in magical technology have emerged from the Goblin engineering guild. The nobles and rulers of the city-state vie for control of the industrial boom. Magic lights, anti-gravity wagons and trains, and other mass manufactured goods flow out of Atania daily.

Ghealdar, rebuilt as a city full of canals and locks, competes with Atania for trade from the East.

The Free States watch their Northern border, wary of what will come out of the Hartland forest and when Gibbard will begin to assert its will in the world again.

And all nations maintain a cautious relationship with the beings of the Feywild. They guard their Runegates carefully, lest any unwelcome Unseelie Fey enter their cities.

The defining trait of Rune is the tension between the mortal realm and the Feywild. There are many portals, called Runegates, between the realms.

Many of the normal conflicts and competitions between mortals are exacerbated by interference from powerful Fey beings who are fascinated by mortals as playthings.
