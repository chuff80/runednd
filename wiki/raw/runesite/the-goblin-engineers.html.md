The Goblin Grok - a former bridge engineer for the fallen minotaur emperor Gabalifix, Grok was an exceptionally intelligent and disciplined goblin. He stole spell books from Empire, taught himself the basics of magic, and organized the goblins into an efficient, militant merchant brigade. His merchant army of approximately 500 goblins took over the Bay of Atania and all of the Empire's ships. They built the first manually powered submarines at the behest of Reconcilers in order to retrieve a piece of Cyric's phylactery.

Over the last 500 years, that original brigade of goblin rebels formed into a formal militarized hierarchy of engineers and wizards. The goblins established themselves as powerful network  of loosely organized merchants and engineers. Their skills rebuilt most of Atania after the Reconciliation, and the Goblins became instrumental in the reclamation and re-founding of Ghealdar.

Immediately after the Reconciliation, several wizards founded the School of Arcana, ostensibly with the goal of teaching humans how to use magic. Many of the early trainees of the school had a strong mistrust of the new relationship between the Lady of the Fey and the mortal realm. While the Lady promised cooperation, a complete dismantling of the Gaeas, and procedures for opening and closing the Runegates, the Engineering School pushed for additional education by Fey wizards.

In later years the School pushed King Darian II for rules around the use of magic. The Accord of Magic and Science established the Registry of Wizards and Artifacts and gave Grok's goblins primary responsibility for administering the regulation of magical items, indirectly leading to the proliferation of enchanted items while also restricting wizards' ability to profit from their own enchantment skills.

Mozetd Boneclub is one of the current Arcana School leaders. A Goblin, he is the master of novices and doesn't take kindly to undisciplined goblins. He was an engineer by trade before he took up the study of arcana, and takes a very practical approach to life, magic, and discipline.
