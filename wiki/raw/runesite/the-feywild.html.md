The new, terrifying Everstorm that has raged across the Northwest corner of the Feywild for the last few weeks has caused havoc. The Summer Queen predicted that Niko's change would bring chaos to the Sidhe Court, but this level of destruction caused the Lady to banish her to the mortal realm out of frustration. The Djinn and the Celestials have begun recruiting Archfey to their sides.

The Archfey:

Baba Yaga - Her last appearance started just outside the Saeserin swamp in her customary magical hut. She flew from there with the Dragon Talkers to Ghealder, where she worked with them to confront and defeat The Raven Queen. No one knows where she went after that. Baba Yaga's motives and goals have always been inscrutable and hard to predict.

Fathaghn, Lord of the Forest - Satyr Archfey whose kingdom contains the great maze where foolish mortals are trapped in the Feywild, and the Summer Queen's enemies are banished. He now houses the Summer Queen's allies and acts as her primary supporter in plots against the Lady. With the Queen's entrance into the Domhaine an Duine, Fathagn's place in the Court of the Lady is uncertain.

Foghlai Mara - The pirate prince Mara is one of the few Unseelie Fey who cares to attend the Court. He frequently spoke on behalf of the Frost Prince, though none of the Fey are certain that the Foghlai Mara had the authority to do so. With Nyx assuming that role, Mara left the Court to sail the seas again, and his boat has disappeared from the Feywild and appeared in Domhaine an Duine, off the coast of Atania.

Niko the Frost Prince - Replacing the former Frost Prince, Nikos has yet to come into his own. The Fey Court went wild when one of their members was killed and replaced. Only the Lady trusts Nikos, and all members of the Court plot to curry favor or to bring him down. He has no interest in court intrigue and hired Nyx the Bard to speak for him at court.

Tinuviel is a Hamadryad Archfey building a kingdom in the destroyed city of Cendriane. While they do not technically hold title, they have laid claim to it and has shown up at the Summer Queen's Court with their amalgam of eladrin, elves, drow, trolls, goblins, satyrs and other supporters. In the 600 years since their reclamation of Cendriane, they have done little to rebuild the city and it has become a home to some of the most unsavory characters in the Feywild, and is said to frequently host drunken revels with the Foghlai Mara.

The Lady - The Queen of the Fey. The Lady worked behind the scenes tirelessly for two centuries to prepare the Seelie for the return of Cyric. She knew he would return and prepared the Fey Court for that event. In order to defeat Cyric and prepare her people for what she sees as a great threat looming, the Lady has assumed the Aspect of Winter, and the Feywild has slowly grown cold. The Lady is currently keeper of the Anail di - the Breath of the Feywild.

The Summer Queen - deposed over 1,000 years ago by The Lady and the Reckoners, the Summer Queen works behind the scenes to convince the Fey Court that the Aspect of Winter, and the destruction of the Feywild lands that come with it, are unnecessary. She has only recently found some success by pointing out the rise of the new Fey Lord, Nikos. When she blamed the Everstorm on The Lady, she was banished to Domhaine an Duine.

Minor Fey Nobles:

King Bronnor - The leader of the Fomorians. A bloodthirsty savage who rules the Fomorians with terror and violence. Not as stupid as he appears, he was lucky to survive his attack on Senaliesse, and now serves Nikos. He seeks information on the whereabouts of the Twice Cursed Fomorian, Vroken.

Cassandra Songcrest - born a mortal elf, Cassandra's power and proximity to Oolong (a powerful Dao) have elevated her to respect and prestige in the Fey court. With the death of Tarkin, the leader of the Bladesingers, Cassandra has become the de facto leader of the elves. She is preparing the elves to return to Domhaine an Duine and to reclaim the Tuatha De Danaan from the Cult of the Raven Queen.

The Game Master - a powerful Fey, also known as Selga, who leads the Wild Hunt, the Game Master will also frequently create elaborate and deadly games in the Maze and in the mortal realm. Also serves as the personal Huntsman to the Lady.

Gridgemoth - the general of Senaliesse, and Consort to the Queen of Summer.

Riardan - Son of the Summer Queen, he betrayed her to the Reckoners. He spends his time at court, away from his mother. He spent some time in the Fomorian tunnels after being kidnapped by them. His rescue by mortals has left him indebted and with a completely different outlook on mortals from the rest of his mother's court. With the banishment of his mother to Domhaine an Duine, he traveled to Atania in order to help the mortals rebuild and keep an eye on his mother.

Rook - an emissary of Fathagn. Previously assisted with the destruction of Razorbeak, a Kenku wizard and known thrall of Cyric the Warlord.

Theobald the Satyr - a Fey bard, an independent actor and unofficial chronicler of the Fey court. Theobald helped the Elves escape into the Feywild.

Whistlefeather - a powerful changeling and servent of The Lady

Important Legends

The Lady of the Fey tells a story of the lost love of the Frost Prince. How he had a powerful love and when he fell in love, the mountains were in perpetual Spring, but he was a jealous lover. One day he heard his love talking, and when he came to find out who she was talking to, she was embarrassed and wouldn’t tell him that she was simply talking to the wind and the lake.

Angered because he thought he was being cheated on, the Frost Prince struck his love. She fled into the woods, and he pursued her. She was swift, but not swift enough. When he caught her, he struck her again and again, until she was nearly dead.

With her last breath, the Frost Prince’s love cursed him that his heart would always be as cold as it was now, and that he would never again know the warmth of a lover’s touch, that everything he touched would turn to ice, and that he would never know love again.

Finally, she revealed to him that she had given birth to a child, his child. The child would never know who he was, would leave the Feywild, and wander the mortal world, a Princess without a kingdom, for it was better to have no history than to be his child, and she would never, ever be found.

It was recently revealed that the descendant of this lover, a being named Laeanna , might be Cyric the Warlord.
