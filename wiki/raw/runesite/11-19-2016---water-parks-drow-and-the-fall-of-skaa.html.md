Sorting through the loot of our previously slain foes, we feel a very large rumbling: A massive earthquake strikes ; the hallway from which we came cracks and slams shut with a massive burst of wind, knocking all of us to the ground. We rouse surrounded by fallen debris, rocks, and dirt, and see a hidden doorway, now visible; it leads to a tunnel going down into the caves that is just large enough as to where Religeuse has to duck his head to fit. Every few seconds or so there is another small tremor, and we see pieces of rock grinding off from one another, falling to the ground.

Following the long hallway, we see various bunks and some dead bodies piled up in the hall; it seems the Kobolds had found this secret dwelling area and took it over before we arrived. Our narrow hallway opens into a wider space: a bit of an open cavern in which we can hear the sound of rushing wind and the deep whoosh of an underground river. There is a single rock bridge situated over the river, and we decide to cross. The river itself has some visible rapids, but is covered in silt. The bride gives way and we fall into the water. The waterbeds have shifted, and now strong currents pull us deeper into the earth – Rapids are everywhere, and all of us fear for our lives. K’nak’likuk turns into an octopus, and quickly casts a spell allowing all of us to breathe underwater – This helps little for our panic, as we bounce, are pulled, and slammed into each other and the earth. We pull together some rope and tie all of us together, hoping our shared weight will keep us a bit more stable, and ride along the river being slammed into every surface the river has.

The water calms, and Kellison hears a very deep pitch of rushing water. The river suddenly becomes very deep. Before Kellison can warn us of what is to come, we are all thrown over the edge of a huge waterfall; emptying into a massive underground cavern. We free fall for a bit until Kellison, Religieuse, and Kari catch a hold of a something to stop our descent. They get stuck on this something and after pulling us onto its net, we notice we have landed in a huge spider web, connected directly to the massive stalagmites overhead . While these sticky webs flexed to absorb our momentum, allowing us to not die – Surely what made these webs would be inclined to fix that. Beau casts a spell on Religieuse, giving him the ability to fly . He carries all of us, angelic, to a rope bride we see and sets us down gently before 4 giant spiders swarm our landing spot.  K’nak’likuk, not wanting to be outdone, turns into a giant wolf spider and walks up the spider web, without getting stuck, to the giant stalagmites overhead.

Part 2: A nice happy town filled with very helpful denizens

The stalagmites are carved into and hollowed out – They are connected by rope bridges, and contain windows and doors. Whatever lives down here sure loves spiders and the darkness. I bet they are super nice. Damaged and terrified, we look in the door closest to us, and it opens into a circular chamber covered in a thick expensive carpets in black and violet patterns that look an awful lot like spider webs. Along the wall we see censors filled with incense with smoke coming out. In the middle of this room is a large statue of a horrifying spider. Directly across from us is another doorway (likely leading to another rope-bridge) and there is a trap door leading to a ladder going down into the tip of the stalagmite. Torg tells us about the spider goddess of the Dark Elves, and then explains that the Dark Elves are not a fun bunch. We decide to go back over the bridge on which we landed and enter the door on the other side. In this room, there is a wooden table set up in the middle of the room, with 4 chairs surrounding it. There is another door, like the other room directly across from it, and behind the table is a large tapestry. Behind this tapestry, we see a leg and a boot. Torg speaks with a gentle, calm voice “ We are lost, mean you no harm, and we just want to leave”. Torg is then immediately hit by two crossbow bolts and is poisoned. At this moment, he fills the room with a fog cloud and rushes back onto the suspension bridge. Cue fight music

A Drow with a shortsword rushes out to attack Torg, who falls unconscious. Beau hits this one with an eldritch blast, and K’nak’likuk seeing an opening, creates a boom of bright moonlight in the entryway to the second stalagmite. Religieuse heals his downed comrade as two additional Drow levitate out of the windows. Bolts fly over Kellison, but she gets hit in the back with another: they have surrounded us on the suspension bridge. Skod hits one of the Drow and Beau throws a fireball at the Drow now behind us. We look and a Drow priestess appears and is flanked by two additional Drow. Religieuse casts fairy fire on them, covering them in Dim light, but making them easier to see. Kari throws a fireball inside their circular room and catches the carpet on fire. The priestess hisses something in a sibilant voice and turns her hand down: thousands of spiders appear underneath the bridge and swarm us . Kellison is bitten unconscious as the spiders crawl into her mouth. We are hit by a lightning bolt, and the ropes of the bridge seem to unravel at our sides. Kari and Beau fall unconscious, and both he and Kellison are revived. K’nak’likuk casts his wind wall, protecting us from projectiles. Religieuse grabs Torg and Beau and makes for the room with the now slain Drow swordsman. The well place wind wall fells the two levitating Drow, as the priestess pulls a dagger from her robes to cut the other rope. Kari falls, screaming, to the spiders below but grabs a rope and swings onto a stalagmite . Juice grabs K’nak’likuk’s hand before he is able to fall, and catches him pulling him into the room. One of the Drow behind the priestess casts a spell into the room in which we are standing that forms black tentacles, grabbing all of us. Skod is knocked unconscious. Kari, exhausted, beaten, and pissed off teleports into the room we are in, grabs the entire necklace of fireballs from Beau, and tosses it across the bridge into the room across from us. The priestess and remaining drow evaporate into flames, and we all stabilize, bloody, and tired on the ground. Religieuse grabs himself another shield and others grab a magical shortsword, some poison that does d6 damage, and some crossbow bolts.

We are left with a few options: Cut the ropes completely surrounding us, block the windows and rest, or continue on, finding a place where there is only one entrance and no one around to see us. We venture forth across the bridge leading out of this room.

Part 3: Finally, rest.

Crossing the bridge, we see a door that has been moderately crushed by the earthquake. Torg scouts ahead to see what he can see, and we climb a large 10ft wide staircase carved into the living rock. To our left is the chasm from whence we came, the water still rushing, with spider webs positioned everywhere. To our right is a giant rock wall. About 60 feet up the steps there is another landing on which we see a small living quarters (a door and a window) carved into the rock face. Torg sneaks up, notices the door is open, and that no one is inside, nor on the landing. Next to this door, about 15 feet back, is a wooden wall with a gate that has not collapsed. As we listen at this gate, we can hear snuffling behind the gate that decidedly is not from a Kobold. We decide this is as good a place as any, and quietly situate ourselves in the living quarters to rest. Pushing a bunk against the door, and having Juice sleep on it, we sleep in shifts. On the Wizards’ shift, they hear sounds outside of the window and door; waking K’nak’likuk to tell him about this, Klick Klack turns into a wolf spider to climb out the window and see: 3 Drow and a giant ape bear stand outside our door. The Drow start chanting, and our room is filled with Webs; Kellison, Beau and Kari are cut free. WE WISH TO SPEAK we respond, hoping they don’t want to easily kill us. They tell us to Throw our weapons down and surrender: We throw out the weapons we found in the previous location, as well as some of our extra ones. They tell us now to exit the room, from the door, slowly. We respond that we can’t, because of all of the webbing. They release the webbing, and cast a magical darkness on the room making it so we can’t see anything. They then tell us to come crawling out, one by one. Religieuse goes out first and is pushed to the ground, all the while saying “This is a bit extreme, I would just like to talk”. Not wanting to go down without a fight, Klick Klack turns into a large warhouse, and Skod mounts for an assault. Cue fight music

Riding out, Skod flings his glaive around hitting the giant bear outside our room three times. Kellison leaps from the room, punching it in its snout, and Beau eldritch blasts it from inside the room. An icestorm is casted by Kari, covering the ground in a slick layer of ice, and he kills the bear. The 3 Drow warriors attack, and a priestess walks up behind them. Torg, using his tactical fighting, peaks out the barracks window, picking off the Drow before hiding again behind the walls. The fight ensues, as Religieuse turtles up, drawing fire. The wooden door slams open, and another bear creature comes charging out. Hitting the ice slick, he loses balance and is coming directly for Skod, and Beau. Beau jumps out of the way and Skod is hit by this charge. The charging creature pushes them both off the side of the cliff. Torg gets poisoned by a priestess, Beau and Kari focus fire the Drow down. Religieuse casts light, allowing us to see better, and allowing the Drow to be drowned in a bright, heavenly, glow. Kellison jumps over the ice patch and attacks the Drow closest to her, killing it. Torg kills the other warrior. K’nak’likuk gets hit and falls unconscious, which is quickly stooped by Religieuse.  Klick, revived, takes out the priestess and all we are left with is a single warrior. Torg misses his shot, and Religeuse blocks his way back into the caves, saying he would just like to talk. The Drow sneers and jumps off the edge of the cliff. Torg shoots his arrows, trailing him into the abyss.

The cliff face to their left is a sheer drop, the door behind leads to the barracks in which they slept, in front of them is the stairs from which they came (leading to no way out), and to the right the large wooden door is now open, leading deeper into the bowels of the earth and the roots of the mountains.
