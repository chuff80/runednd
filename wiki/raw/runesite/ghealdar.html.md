Ghealdar is now a city flooded by the massive Great River. The Dump, former home to the free goblins, and many of the riverside homes of the merchant houses, is completely underwater. Most of the city was killed in the flood and the battle with the Raven Queen. Many of the survivors say that the Curse of Badb was never truly lifted and that no one should remain in Ghealdar. Those whose houses were completely submerged have either moved to the North of the city or fled to the settle on the Thir or in the Free States.

The only remaining governing power is the Gibard orcs, and they are slowly withdrawing in order to secure their kingdom. In the next few weeks, the City Council expects the orcs to be gone. What will fill the power vacuum remains to the be seen.

A brief history of Ghealdar

The second largest city in Domhaine an Duine, Ghealder was left a smoking ruin after the Hartland war. Cyric the Warlord devastated the Fey Court's Eladrin army there and the Summer Queen retaliated by sending her most powerful general, Badb, to decimate the city. Cyric took advantage of the hundreds of newly dead by enslaving their bodies and spirits in horrific undeath.

For 200 years after Cyric was brought down, the city laid abandoned. The tribes of the plains avoided the city with the exception of the handful of foolhardy warriors on vision quests.

To reclaim their ancestral home, The Reconcilers led a coalition of forces from the newly established Free States to cleanse the city of the planar rift leading to the Shadowfell. After that, the city thrived under the leadership of a diverse council. The city became a marvelous meld of magic and mechanics, built on the backs of thousands of goblin slaves captured in the Dragonspine mountains.

Six years ago, a group of goblins led a city-wide protest against slavery. The official story is that the goblins found a sympathetic ally in Rutherford, a wealthy and powerful gnomish wizard. Rutherford, in his final days, pushed for the release of goblins from the bonds of slavery. His dying wishes were honored by the city council, but that decision has resulted in disaster. The unofficial story is only whispered but involves a team of goblin covert operatives forcing the city council's hand through blackmail.

The goblins were immediately given their freedom. No more slavery. A large percentage left, some going to Atania and the Free States, until these nations closed their border to goblin refugees. Some returned to their ancestral homeland in the mountains only to discover their feral cousins were not as welcoming. Those goblins ended up becoming sharecroppers in Gibard, the Orcish kingdom, or setting up their own camps in the rugged mountains of the Dragonspine.

Of those who stayed, and survived the Elemental Apocalypse, an enterprising group organized a system of transportation by gondola. Seizing the first-mover advantage, the goblins now wholly own the best and easiest means of transportation throughout the city. It's only been a few weeks, so it remains to be seen whether they can keep ahold of that monopoly.

Before the Elemental Apocalypse and the war with the Raven Queen, there had been some talk of giving goblins education and land, but nothing was organized. Many shopkeepers closed up, claiming they could not pay their former goblin slaves. Many goblins are poorer now because they no longer have benefactors, the Dump is now underwater, and gondola poling doesn't pay much.

Of the wealthy families that were slaveholders, several of them left the city out of fear of retribution. Their absence caused a cascade of business failures. The orcs already in Ghealdar tried to step in to fill those gaps, but the Tuatha Dé, a secret cabal of elven freedom fighters, began quietly undermining and damaging those businesses. A handful of goblins have prospered, but most of them have no education and have succumbed to despair or lives of crime.

Important NPCs & organizations

The Tuatha Dé Danaan

The elves who have fought guerrilla warfare against Gibard saw an opportunity when Ghealdar’s economy collapsed and began attacking orcish business interests throughout Ghealdar. After it was revealed that the Tuatha De had pledged themselves to the Raven Queen, any elf in Ghealdar is immediately arrested and imprisoned by the occupying Gibard army.

The Ghealdar City Council

James Sundown , a devout human leader, James brings a strong religious bent to the Council. While he was tempted to enter the priesthood, his family was powerfully connected politically and he decided that he could do more good by leading the city and keeping the church's influence subtly present. He managed to survive both the flood and the Raven Queen's vengeance. He is the apparent leader of the council, but the destruction of his family's home by the river has left him virtually penniless and holding his influence together by sheer will alone.

Tal’Eowan is an elven former blade singer, now a member of the city council, the only one to survive the collapse of the city’s economy. She was rumored to traffic in the drug trade but those rumors have faded as she has worked tirelessly to improve the lives of average Ghealdarians.

The council is considering replacing those who died, but that is still to be determined.

Other NPCs

Aaron Featherington was one of Seamus' direct reports. He lost his job when Seamus fell on hard times. He has drifted from master to master looking for work, dabbling in a protection racket that keeps a small sector of Ghealdar free of violence.

Finn Aero is a human fence who made it through the upheaval by working with nearly everyone, laundering antiquities and other hot goods. Finn is a mild-mannered character, but he is rumored to have dealings with privateers who work for Atania. The shipyard thieves guild, The Sharks , takes a cut of most of the trade in and out of the port, and in the city, Meirlich is a tight organization of thieves who do high-profile cons on unsuspecting folks. Both groups leverage Finn's connections to have high-powered lawyers that enforce contracts and get their people out of jail.

Sadavir Blessedarmor, Asher, and Cog took over an inn near the Dump. Sadavir fell into a drunken stupor and Asher disappeared with Cog in search of his ancestor, Darian Bloodstone.

Notables killed in the Elemental Apocalypse:

Faile Ba'Shir , a half-elf whose elven heritage hails from Kjeldor, Faile is a good friend of Tal'Eowan, and frequently advocates for elven rights. She handles the burgeoning social justice movement in Ghealdar. Her wealth comes from shrewd investments in trade caravans with Atania and other towns. One of the few whose wealth did not take a hit after the goblins were freed, her power has increased.

Chase Airgid Mac Tire was a human who saw the racial diversity of Ghealdar as a problem. The goblins were slaves, as they should be, but the mix of all of the other races is creating a world that doesn't recognize the dangers of The Fey. Chase openly admired the history of Cyric the Warlord, and he advocated the closure of the Rune Gates to the Feywild. His family wealth came from shipping, which took a huge hit when they could no longer keep goblin slave sailors.

Seamus O’Connell was one of the most prominent slaveholders.

Zel - a young adult black dragon who previously lived underneath Rutherford’s mansion but now occupies a swamp miles East of the city. This dragon has mysteriously allied itself with a handful of goblin criminals, protecting them from law enforcement.

The Ghealdar Goblin Underground Freedom Force

A group of goblins and goblin allies who are working to help Ghealdarian goblins find education and economic prosperity.

Sarge is the twin brother of Religieuse. They're enormous goblins. Where Religieuse is fat and clumsy, Sarge is muscular underneath his layer of fat. He was a slave owned by one of Tal'Eowan's captains, a human named Sledge. Sarge acts as a bouncer for Sledge's night clubs.

Mhamo is the goblin grandmother. She is a mysterious figure who used to arrange for goblins to disappear from Ghealdar, and now helps them sneak into neighboring countries. She is an old, wrinkled goblin that very few people have actually seen.

Grok us the pseudonym used by a loose group of goblin engineers and wizards from Atania who are trying to help Mhamo get the Ghealdarian goblins out or to find resources. The magic devices they previously built for freedom fighting have been lost or sold. They have recently reappeared in several high-profile attacks against the orcs.

Filch is part of a street gang run by Shadow. He's a consummate sneak. He can infiltrate anything – probably with the help of some kind of magic. His street gang helps Mhamo – but Shadow has secret connections to the biggest crime boss, Finn Aero. Shadow is Finn's slave, and Finn pays her very, very well for her information.
