We agreed with Rutherford that we would take Beau (new teenage goblin friend) back home, and we have a month to do so (and come back). Before we do this, however, we wanted to get some tabs on where Elm is (as he is no longer at the house), and who hired that merc to come attack us in the warehouse we were last in.

Scene 1: Is That A Bear Over There?

The guards are on high patrol, and we head to the Warrens only to find that they are largely being burnt to the ground. Guards are everywhere, and we now have to sneak in and out of a drainage pipe to get there. We just barely make it into the drainage pipe, with some help from ClickClack turning into a dog.

Inside the Warrens, we run into Sarge who said he needs our help: Some other slave masters have been coming through the warrens with their guards, and just taking goblins (with no regard as to who they belong to) saying that they are now their property. That said, they have taken a handful of Goblins to a house he has some eyes on, and would like us to rescue them. In exchange for helping him, we ask him to get an idea as to where Elm may be, and he says he would.

We sneak back out of the same drainage pipe, but the guard doesn't take the bait. ClickClack turns into a bear and chews through the guard's throat right in front of a female monk goblin. The guard manages to yell before his demise, and the horns start going.

Scene 2: We Can Get Away Over ... Nope

The horns kick off a mad scramble through the city. Kellison decides to join the party because, well, she's in on it anyway. Torg deftly leads the party into being surrounded by the city guard. A fight ensues and the party racks up its guard count yet again, but one mage gets away in the clear. (Really!? There's always one that gets away!)

Scene 3: That's Totally A Friendly Deadly Elf Sneaking Up On Our Party

Sarge leads us to a house that we case out: 2 guards inside on the main floor, can't see anything else. We go in through the back door, take out one of the guards (George!), and then open the door that myself and Sarge couldn't knock down (sturdy door... we were going to pincer attack them). The other guard runs around the corner into a kitchen and then down some stairs.

Our party goes into the kitchen, less Beau and ClickClack and we start talking to the guard who is downstairs: Apparently there are goblin slaves being held in the basement with a small group of guards (about 5-8). During this time an elf appears out of nowhere in the main room asking Click and Beau to leave the house, acting like he is going to kill the guards for them. They leave. (Sigh. Yes, that's how it happened.)

Elf guy goes into the kitchen and casts an amazing icy spell that leaves all of us in single digit health points, they rush in to try to help, and a fight breaks out. We in the kitchen (Skod, Torg, Sarge, and Kellison) quickly fall unconscious, and the guards from downstairs charge up and tie us. Scary elf wizard asks who brought us here, and what we are doing, and we say that we are saving goblin slaves. He asks who we belong to, and we tell him Rutherford. He then gets super contemplative and thinks out loud "why would Rutherford want to free slaves?" etc. His name is Oni. He then asks us to work for him, he will buy out our contracts. This dude seems like bad news, so we disagree (in so many words). Click Clack and Beau, and we are brought down to the basement.
